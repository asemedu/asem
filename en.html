About us

ASEM — Association for Supporting Medical Education (EduMed)

A non-profit founded in 2024 in Iași, Romania, by two medical students and one computer-science student.

We train medical-student volunteers to teach first aid and health education in schools across Moldova region (north-east Romania) — hands-on, age-appropriate, with equipment that stays in the schools.

Impact

  • 2,800+ direct beneficiaries in 2025 across three projects in Iași, Bacău and Vaslui counties
  • 372 students, many with special educational needs, in 5 schools in 2026 (interim)
  • 63 RON per direct beneficiary in our 2025 Bârlad project

Programmes

  • first aid for high-school students
  • health education for middle-school and pre-school children
  • inclusive medical education for students with special educational needs
  • goMed, an AR first-aid web app with real-time, on-device CPR posture and rhythm feedback
  • career guidance for rural students

Funded by

Kaufland Romania (Start ONG), Lidl Romania (via the Iași and Bacău Community Foundations), PEPCO Romania, the European Solidarity Corps (EU).
